🛟 Grower Billing — Data Recovery

How this works

The billing app keeps a backup of each grower/month on every device that was used to enter or view it. Below is every backup stored on this device, compared with what the cloud currently has. If a backup here has the entries the cloud lost, click Restore to cloud. Nothing is changed until you click a restore button and confirm.

Open this page on the device/browser your employee used to type the entries — that device holds the most complete backup.
